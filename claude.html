7-Day Creative Challenge

The Original Thinker

One small prompt per day. No experience required.

How this works: Each day has one weird, low-stakes prompt. There's no right answer — in fact, the more unexpected your response, the better. The goal isn't to be "creative." It's to notice when your brain reaches for the obvious answer... and then take one step sideways from it. Spend just 5–10 minutes per day.
